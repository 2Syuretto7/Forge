# Capstone: a calibrated spam filter

The starter generates 20,000 messages, a third of them spam, in which certain words come in phrases. Train naive Bayes with Laplace smoothing on 12,000, fit a temperature on 4,000, and report accuracy and expected calibration error on the last 4,000, before and after calibration. The filter must reach 95 percent accuracy and, after calibration, an error below 0.015. Then write a short note on where the independence assumption fails in this data and how you can see it.
