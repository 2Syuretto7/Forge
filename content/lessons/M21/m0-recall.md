# Recall bridge: from counting to probability

M08 computed probabilities by counting equally likely outcomes: the chance of a full house, of a shared birthday. That works when every outcome is equally likely and finitely many. This lesson keeps the counting picture as a guide and drops both assumptions, then asks the question that matters most in practice: how should a probability change when you learn something? ## What carries over

Probability is still a share of the whole, as in M08. Conditioning restricts the whole: the share of the ill among those who tested positive, not among everyone.
