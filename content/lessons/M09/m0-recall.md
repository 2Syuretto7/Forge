## What carries over

In the plane, the distance from the origin to $(x, y)$ is $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$, and the angle between two arrows decides whether they point the same way. Both ideas extend to any number of coordinates, and in 300 dimensions they are the only way to compare word embeddings, which have no axis anyone can look at.
