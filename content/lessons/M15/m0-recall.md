# Recall bridge: from sequences to functions

M05 defined the limit of a sequence: $a_n \to L$ if for every tolerance $\varepsilon$ the terms eventually stay within $\varepsilon$ of $L$. M06 built $e^x$ as a limit of compounding and as a series. This lesson lets the input vary continuously instead of in steps, and the most important limit in mathematics appears: the slope of a curve at a point. Clear your due cards above first.

## What carries over

The definition of a limit, "for every tolerance there is a threshold", carries over word for word. Only the threshold changes: instead of "for all $n$ beyond $N$" it becomes "for all $x$ within $\delta$ of $a$".
