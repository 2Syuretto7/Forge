# Recall bridge: from one average to many

M22 showed that the average of $n$ independent copies of a variable with variance $\sigma^2$ has variance $\sigma^2/n$. This lesson takes that one line as far as it goes: the average converges to the mean, it does so along a bell curve whatever the shape of the original distribution, and every integral, expectation and probability can be computed by averaging random samples, with an error bar that comes for free. Clear your due cards above first.
