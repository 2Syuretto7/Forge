# Recall bridge: when Ax = b has no solution

M11 classified every linear system: none, one or infinitely many solutions. Fitting data is almost always in the first case: fifty equations, two unknowns, and no line passes through every point. M02 met the same problem at the end of its Scale movement and left it here. This lesson finds the next best thing, and the answer is geometry from M09: a projection. Clear your due cards above first.

## What carries over

$Ax$ ranges over the column space of $A$. When $b$ is not in it, ask for the point of the column space nearest to $b$. M09 found the nearest point on a line by dropping a perpendicular; here the line becomes a plane, or a subspace of any dimension.
