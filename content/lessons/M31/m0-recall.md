# Recall bridge: from likely to guaranteed

M24 bounded deviations with Chebyshev's inequality and approximated them with the central limit theorem; M25 put intervals on estimates. Both were about one estimate at a time, and the CLT's intervals are approximations. This lesson asks for guarantees: how far can an average stray, for certain, and how does that change when you evaluate many models, or a whole class of them, on the same data?
