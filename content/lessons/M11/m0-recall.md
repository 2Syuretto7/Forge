# Recall bridge: from two equations to many

M02 solved two equations in two unknowns by elimination and found one number, the determinant $ad - bc$, that decides whether the solution is unique. M10 read the same system as a matrix equation, $Ax = b$, and saw a zero determinant flatten the plane so that no inverse exists. This lesson does both for any number of equations. ## What carries over

Adding a multiple of one equation to another does not change the solutions. That single fact, applied systematically, solves every linear system and tells you when there is nothing, or too much, to find.
