# Build: powers, e, the logarithm, and softmax

You will compute everything from scratch, using only `+`, `-`, `*` and `/`: integer powers, roots by bisection, $e$ three ways, $e^x$ by its series, the logarithm by inverting $e^x$, and then the functions built from them. Comparing with Python's `math` module is allowed in your experiments; the tests forbid nothing, but you learn the most by not peeking.
