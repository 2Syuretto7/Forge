## What you already know about powers

$2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2$: an integer power is repeated multiplication. Two rules follow by counting factors: $a^m a^n = a^{m+n}$ and $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$. In M01 you used them for powers of ten and found that keeping them true forced $10^0 = 1$ and $10^{-n} = 1/10^n$. This lesson keeps extending the same rules until the exponent can be any real number.
