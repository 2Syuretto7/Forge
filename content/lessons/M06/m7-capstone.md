# Capstone: a growth-law detector

Six datasets, each generated by one of three laws (linear, exponential, power) with noise, with unknown parameters. Plot them in your head on three kinds of axes, and let a computer do the same: fit a straight line in each coordinate system, keep the one that fits best, and read the parameters off its slope and intercept.

:::exercise{ref=capstone}
:::

## When you finish

You can now tell exponential growth from a power law and read either off a plot. M07 takes the exponential into the complex plane, where $e^{i\theta}$ turns instead of growing. M15 shows that $e^x$ is its own derivative. M26 builds floating point from the bits up, and M27 measures information in logarithms. In L11 and M35 the power laws become scaling laws for compute.
