# Capstone: a step-checked algebra notebook

The starter holds a notebook of fifteen short derivations, each a list of expressions where every expression should equal the one before. Thirteen are correct. Two contain one planted error each, the kind of slip that survives a quick read. Your job is to build the checker and find them.

This is how a careful mathematician works: not by trusting each line, but by checking it, cheaply, every time.

:::

## When you finish

Your checker is evidence, not proof: it would accept a step that is wrong only at a few points. M03 makes the difference between testing and proving precise, and gives you the tools to close it. In M04 the expressions become functions you can compose and invert, and in M11 elimination grows into an algorithm for systems of any size.

:::edge{to=M04 text="an expression in x is a function: a machine that turns each input into one output."}
:::
