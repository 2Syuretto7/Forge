# Recall bridge: numbers you can hold

M01 gave you a ladder of powers of ten, units that cancel, and estimates built as products of factors. This lesson gives the factors names and solves for them. Answer the review cards from M01 above before reading on (they appear once M01 is complete and its cards are due).

## Two facts to bring along

**Units behave like symbols.** In M01 you multiplied $60\ \text{km/h}$ by $3\ \text{h}$ and the hours cancelled. You were already doing algebra: treating a symbol (h) as a quantity that obeys the rules of multiplication without knowing what it "is". This lesson does the same with unknown numbers.

**An estimate has an unknown in it.** A Fermi estimate of GPU-hours had the form

$$
\text{hours} = \frac{6 N D}{\text{rate} \times 3600} .
$$

If you know the hours a run took, and $N$, $D$ and the rate's peak, the utilization is the one unknown left, and you can solve for it. That move, from "a formula that gives an answer" to "an equation that pins down an unknown", is algebra.

## A warm-up

Before any rules: if $3$ boxes plus $2$ loose apples make $11$ apples, how many apples are in a box? You can answer without symbols (take away the loose 2, share 9 among 3 boxes, so 3). Algebra is that reasoning written down so it works when the numbers are not friendly, when there are two unknowns at once, and when there are a thousand.
