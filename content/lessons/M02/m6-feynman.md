# Explain it plainly: doing the same thing to both sides

You have derived the rules, built a checker, and broken the rules on purpose. Explain the central idea to a 12-year-old who knows arithmetic but has never solved an equation.

Guidelines:

- Write 150 to 400 words.
- Use the picture of a balance, or another of your own, and one concrete equation.
- Say both why the rule works and exactly when it fails.
- Avoid the words "field" and "inverse" unless you explain them.
