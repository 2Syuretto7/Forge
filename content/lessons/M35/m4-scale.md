With Chinchilla's exponents, a compute budget ten times larger should go into a model how many times larger?

:::reveal
About $2.83$: $10^{0.452}$. The data grow by $10^{0.548} \approx 3.5$. Both grow by about the square root of the extra compute, which is the Chinchilla prescription; earlier fits, with a larger exponent for $N$, recommended growing models much faster than data.
