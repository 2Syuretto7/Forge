# Recall bridge: from one random draw to a random walk

M22 described a random variable by its distribution and computed expectations and variances; M13 found eigenvectors and closed with a chain whose long-run distribution is the eigenvector of eigenvalue 1. This lesson joins the two. A sequence of random states, each depending only on the one before, settles into that eigenvector, and running such a chain is the most general way known to draw samples from a distribution you can only write down up to a constant. Clear your due cards above first.
