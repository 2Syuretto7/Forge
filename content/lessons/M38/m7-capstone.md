# Capstone: the mathematics of a real paper

Choose one paper. Write its central derivation step by step, naming the lesson behind each step; reproduce its central claim in NumPy with the code you built in this lesson; and map every lesson the derivation used and every edge of the curriculum graph it travelled. The tests check the reproduction against the paper's claim and every lesson and edge against the graph.

You have used the whole track on real research: the derivations, the reproductions and the breaks are the same moves the papers' authors made. L20 audits a capstone model with the same method.
