# Explain it plainly: a finite sum of infinitely many terms

Explain to a 12-year-old how adding infinitely many numbers can give a finite answer, without using the word "limit".

Guidelines:

- Write 150 to 400 words.
- Use a concrete picture: walking halfway to a wall again and again, cutting a cake in halves, a bouncing ball.
- Say what the "answer" of an infinite sum means.
- Explain why $1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \dots$ is different.
