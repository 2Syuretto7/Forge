## Where you are

In M03's break-it movement, an argument by steps "proved" that all horses are the same colour. Its step from $n$ to $n + 1$ worked for every $n \geq 2$ but failed at $n = 1$. The repair is not to abandon arguments by steps but to state exactly what they need. That statement is induction, and it is the first thing this lesson proves you can trust.

In M04 you met error laws like $C/n^2$: a function of $n$, the number of pieces, that shrinks as $n$ grows. That is a sequence, and "shrinks toward 0" is a limit. This lesson makes both words precise.
