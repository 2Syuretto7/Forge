# Recall bridge: from vectors to machines that move them

M09 gave vectors lengths and angles, measured them with dot products, and projected one onto another. The projection $b \mapsto \frac{a \cdot b}{a \cdot a} a$ was already a machine that takes a vector in and gives a vector out. This lesson studies such machines when they respect addition and scaling: they are exactly the matrices. Clear your due cards above first.

:::

## What carries over

Every output coordinate of a matrix-vector product is a dot product, so everything M09 said about dot products (symmetry, linearity, Cauchy-Schwarz) applies one row at a time.
