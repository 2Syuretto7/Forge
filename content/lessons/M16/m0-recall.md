# Recall bridge: from slopes to curvature

M15 defined the derivative as the slope of the best linear approximation, and measured how finite differences lose to rounding. A line captures where a function is going; it cannot say whether the function is about to turn. This lesson adds the second derivative, then all the others, and uses them to approximate functions, find their minima, and solve equations fast. Clear your due cards above first.

## What carries over

The difference quotient error of M15 was $\frac{h}{2}|f''|$: the second derivative already appeared, measuring how far a function bends away from its tangent. Here it moves to the centre.
