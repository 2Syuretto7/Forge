# Recall bridge: from rates to totals

M15 took a function and found its rate of change. This lesson goes the other way: given a rate, find the total it accumulates. The two operations turn out to be inverses, which is the fundamental theorem of calculus. M05's series return too: an integral is a limit of sums, as a series is a limit of partial sums. ## What carries over

Every differentiation rule, read backwards, is an integration rule. The finite-difference trade-off of M15 returns as the choice of how many points a quadrature rule uses.
