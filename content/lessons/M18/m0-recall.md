# Recall bridge: derivatives meet matrices

M16 approximated a function of one variable by its tangent line and, one order further, by a parabola. M10 showed that the linear maps between spaces of any dimension are matrices. Put the two together: a function of many variables, near a point, looks like a linear map, and that map is a matrix of partial derivatives. Clear your due cards above first.

## What carries over

M15's definition, "the error of the linear approximation shrinks faster than the step", carries over word for word. Only the slope changes: from a number to a vector, then to a matrix.
