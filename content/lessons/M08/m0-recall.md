## Why count without listing

Listing works for small sets: the 8 functions from a 3-element set to a 2-element set can be written out. It stops working fast. The orderings of a 52-card deck number about $8 \times 10^{67}$, more than the atoms in the Earth. Counting them takes one line. M01's ladder of powers of ten tops out around $10^{23}$; counting will produce numbers no ladder can hold, and logarithms (M06) are how you will handle them.
