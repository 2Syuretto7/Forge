# Capstone: exact against simulated

Ten probability puzzles, each solved two ways: exactly, by counting, and approximately, by simulation. If your counting is right, the two agree to within a few standard errors; if they do not, one of them is wrong, and the standard error tells you whether the gap is luck. Then size a hash for a deduplication pipeline.
