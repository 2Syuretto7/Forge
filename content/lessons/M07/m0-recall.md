## A pattern you have seen twice

Every time the numbers have been extended (negatives, fractions, reals in M01; negative and fractional exponents in M06), the rule was the same: add new numbers where an old question had no answer, and keep every old rule working. The question this time is $x^2 = -1$. The answer, $i$, turns out to be a quarter turn of the plane.
