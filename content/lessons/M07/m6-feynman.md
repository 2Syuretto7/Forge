# Explain it plainly: a quarter turn

Explain to a 12-year-old why multiplying by $i$ is a quarter turn.

Guidelines:

- Write 150 to 400 words.
- Start from the number line and what multiplying by $-1$ does to it.
- Use one concrete point, such as $3 + 4i$, and say where it goes.
- Say why doing it twice gives $-1$.
